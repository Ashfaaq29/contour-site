Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

CONTOUR is a free puzzle game by Oddmatter Games. We do not sell your personal data. The game is paid for by optional ads (shown by Google AdMob) and optional in-app purchases. Your progress stays on your phone; Play Together is the only feature that stores anything about you online.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Oddmatter Games, run by Ashfaaq Jowaheer, Republic of Mauritius. Contact: oddmattergames@gmail.com.

Your age

The first time you open CONTOUR it asks for your year of birth. Only an age range (under 13, 13 to 15, or 16 and over) is kept, on your phone; your birth year isn't stored or sent anywhere. The range is used to:

  • Under 13: show only child-directed ads (no personalisation, no advertising identifier, family-safe content) and switch off Play Together.
  • Under 16: never personalise ads where the law requires consent.

What the app stores on your phone

Your progress (points, rank, coins, Expedition chapters, Daily Challenge history), your settings, your age range and a record of completed purchases are saved in a file on your device. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Play Together (optional, 13 and over)

Opening Play Together creates an anonymous online account: no email, phone number or password. It stores only what the features need:

  • Your surveyor name and tag (e.g. Ana#4821), which other players can see, when you were last online and what you are doing in the game (for example "Surveying Lone Peak"), which your friends can see.
  • Your friends list and friend requests, players you block, match invites, live match lobbies and their results, and friend challenges with their scores.
  • Reports you send about other players (the reason and their name), which we review to keep Play Together safe.
  • A backup copy of your game progress, only if you press Back up or finish a session while online.

During a live match, your picks, compass direction, map pings and fixed quick-chat phrases are relayed to the other player and not stored. There is no free-text chat and no voice. Names are checked for offensive words. This data is hosted by Supabase on servers in the European Union (Ireland).

Ads (Google AdMob)

CONTOUR shows optional reward videos (free coins, a free hint, a streak freeze or a doubled Daily bonus, a few a day) and an occasional full-screen ad between Quick Play rounds (never in a player's first sessions, the Daily Challenge, Expeditions, live matches or challenges; Remove Ads turns these off). To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as your advertising identifier, IP address and ad interactions, under Google's privacy policy. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and some US states), the game asks for your choice first using Google's consent message, and you can change it any time under Settings → Purchases & privacy → Privacy choices. On iPhone, ads are personalised only if you allow tracking in Apple's prompt. If you decline, you still see ads, but they aren't based on your activity.

Purchases

Coin packs, the Starter Pack and Remove Ads are bought through Google Play or the App Store. They handle the payment; we never see your card or store account details. We receive only the purchase's product and transaction ID, kept on your device so nothing is granted twice.

Why we use your data (legal bases)

  • To provide the game and Play Together, and to deliver purchases: performance of our agreement with you (Terms of Service).
  • Personalised ads: your consent, where the law requires it. You can withdraw it at any time in Privacy choices.
  • Non-personalised ads, safety (names, blocks, reports) and preventing fraud and cheating: our legitimate interests in keeping the game free, safe and fair.
  • Keeping records the law requires: legal obligation.

How long we keep it

Play Together data is kept until you delete your online account, then removed. Reports are kept only as long as needed for safety reviews; if your account is deleted, reports you sent are kept without your name. Data on your phone is kept until you delete the app.

Who receives it

Google (AdMob ads, and Google Play for purchases), Apple (App Store purchases and tracking choice), and Supabase (hosting for Play Together), each only for the purposes above. Google may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under its own safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. We don't share your data with anyone else, except if the law requires it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, object to or restrict how it's used, and withdraw consent. Most of this you can do yourself in the game:

  • Delete: Settings → Online account → Delete online account, or request deletion by email.
  • Change your name: Settings → Surveyor name.
  • Ad choices: Settings → Purchases & privacy → Privacy choices.

For anything else, email us with your surveyor tag; we reply within 30 days. You can also complain to a data protection authority, such as the Data Protection Office of Mauritius or the authority where you live.

Children

CONTOUR is not directed at children, but anyone can play. Players who enter an age under 13 see only child-directed ads and can't use Play Together, so no online account is created for them. If you believe a child under 13 has a Play Together account, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data sent between the game and our servers is encrypted in transit. Online data can only be read or changed through checked server functions, and each player can only access their own private data.

What the app does not do

  • No email, phone number, real-name or social-network sign-in.
  • No analytics service of our own; game events are only written to the device's log.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.
  • Apart from ads, purchases and Play Together, no internet connection is used.

Changes

If this changes, this page will be updated before the change ships, and the store privacy details (App Store privacy label, Google Play data safety) will be updated to match.
