Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

CONTOUR is a free puzzle game that works on your device. We do not sell your personal data. The game is paid for by optional ads (shown by Google AdMob) and optional in-app purchases, described below. Your progress stays on your phone; Play Together is the only feature that stores anything online.

What the app stores

Your progress (points, rank, Expedition chapters, Daily Challenge history) and your settings are saved in a file on your device only. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Play Together (optional)

Opening Play Together creates an anonymous online account: no email, phone number or password. It stores only what the features need:

  • Your surveyor name and tag (e.g. Ana#4821), which other players can see, and when you were last online.
  • Players you block, and reports you send about other players (the reason and their name), which Oddmatter Games reviews to keep Play Together safe.
  • Your friends list and friend requests, match invites, live match lobbies and their results, and friend challenges with their scores.
  • A backup copy of your game progress, only if you press Back up or finish a session while online.

During a live match, your picks, compass direction, map pings and quick-chat phrases are relayed to the other player and are not stored. There is no free-text chat and no voice. This data is held by our hosting provider, Supabase (servers in the EU, Ireland), only to run these features.

Delete it any time: Settings → Online account → Delete online account permanently removes your account and everything listed above. Your progress on the phone is kept. Without the app, you can request deletion by email.

Ads (Google AdMob)

CONTOUR shows two kinds of ads, both from Google AdMob:

  • Videos you choose to watch for a reward (free coins, a free hint, a streak freeze, or a doubled Daily bonus). Limited to a few a day.
  • An occasional full-screen ad between Quick Play rounds, never in a player's first sessions and never in the Daily Challenge, Expeditions, live matches or challenges. Remove Ads turns these off.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as your advertising identifier, IP address and ad interactions, under Google's privacy policy. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) the game asks for your choice first, using Google's consent form, and you can change it any time under Settings → Purchases & privacy → Privacy choices. On iPhone, ads are personalised only if you allow tracking in Apple's prompt; you can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not based on your activity.

Purchases

Coin packs, the Starter Pack and Remove Ads are bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment; we never see your card or account details. The game keeps a record of completed purchase IDs on your device so nothing is granted twice. One-time purchases can be restored with Restore purchases.

What the app does not do

  • No email, phone number, real-name or social-network sign-in.
  • No analytics service of our own; game events are only written to the device's log.
  • Apart from ads, purchases and Play Together, no internet connection is used. Terrain Duel codes are short codes you share yourself; the game turns them into the same land on each device without any network access.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Children

CONTOUR is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. Play Together uses fixed quick-chat phrases only (no free text), and players are identified only by the name they choose.

Third parties

The game is built with the Unity engine. It uses Google AdMob for ads (see above), Unity In-App Purchasing to talk to the App Store and Google Play, and Supabase for Play Together. Unity's analytics, crash and performance reporting are turned off. Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated App Store statistics (such as download counts) under Apple's own privacy policy; these do not identify you.

Changes

If this changes, this page will be updated before the change ships, and the store privacy details (App Store privacy label, Google Play data safety) will be updated to match.
