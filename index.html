How to play

The top card shows the view from where you stand: drag to look around. The contour map below shows three points, A, B and C. Compare the skyline with the map (summits, ridges, valleys), pick the point you are standing on and lock it in. The result screen explains the answer with three clues, and you can turn the 3D board with your fingers.

Common questions

Does CONTOUR need the internet? The puzzles work offline. Ads, purchases and Play Together (live matches, friends and challenges) need a connection.

Where is my progress saved? On your device. If you use Play Together you can also back it up online (Settings → Online account → Back up) and restore it on a new phone.

Is the game free? Yes. It is supported by ads and optional purchases: Compass Coin packs, a one-time Starter Pack, and Remove Ads, which turns off the ads between rounds. Reward videos (free coins, a free hint, a streak freeze) are always optional.

I bought something but don't have it. Open the coin shop (tap your coins) or Settings → Purchases & privacy and tap Restore purchases. If that doesn't help, email us with the date of the purchase; refunds are handled by the App Store or Google Play.

How do I change my ad privacy choices? Settings → Purchases & privacy → Privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). On iPhone you can also change tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

How do I delete my online data? See Delete your account below.

Someone has an offensive name or is bothering me. In Play Together, tap the ⋯ button next to their name and choose Report or Block. Blocked players can't add you, invite you or join your matches. You can unblock them in Settings → Online account → Blocked players.

The music is too loud or quiet. Settings → Audio has separate Music, Ambience and Effects sliders.

Delete your account

CONTOUR's online features (Play Together) use an anonymous account with your surveyor name and tag. You can delete it and everything linked to it at any time.

In the app: Settings → Online account → Delete online account. Deletion is immediate.

Without the app (for example if you've uninstalled it): email oddmattergames@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my CONTOUR account" and your surveyor tag (for example Ana#4821). We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted: your name and tag, friends and friend requests, blocks, match invites and results, friend challenges and your scores, and your online progress backup. Reports you sent are kept without your name for safety reviews. Nothing is kept after deletion except where the law requires it. Progress saved on your phone is not affected; uninstalling the app removes it.
